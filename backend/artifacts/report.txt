CAVEATS
  * Small data: a few hundred training storms. Treat differences inside the confidence intervals as noise.
  * Forecasts are issued from best-track positions (post-analysed), not real-time advisories, so real-time skill will be lower.
  * No environmental fields (SST, shear, steering flow) are used yet - those need ERA5 (a later step).
  * Not an operational forecast; does not replace IMD advisories.
